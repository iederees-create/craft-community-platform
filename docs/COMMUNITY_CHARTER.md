# Tuftlings Community Charter

Status: Draft for founding-member review  
Owner: Iederees Francis / NextGen Webs  
Applies to: Tuftlings community platform, Pattern Lab, circles, challenges, project galleries, comments, and help spaces

## Purpose

Tuftlings exists to help makers finish projects, learn skills, share useful knowledge, and build on ideas with clear permission and credit. It is a craft community, not an attention marketplace.

## Product principles

1. Projects before popularity. Creations, process notes, and useful help are the primary objects—not follower totals.
2. Finite by design. Feeds are chronological and paginated. There is no infinite scroll, public streak, or engagement bait.
3. Credit travels with a remix. A remix must preserve the original project's attribution and declared permission.
4. Permission is explicit. No licence choice means showcase only.
5. Helpful context beats vague reactions. Structured Make Logs and step-specific help are encouraged.
6. Safety and dignity are product requirements. Reporting, blocking, moderation, and privacy controls are part of the core experience.
7. Humans remain accountable. Automated tools may assist, but they do not make final moderation, pattern-safety, or publishing decisions.
8. Honest status. Prototype, testing, approved, published, and retired pattern states must never be blurred.

## Member commitments

Members agree to:

- Share only content they own or are authorised to share.
- Credit patterns, designers, and source projects.
- Respect the selected remix permission.
- Use constructive, specific feedback.
- Add meaningful alt text to creation images.
- Avoid publishing private order details, addresses, emails, medical information, or other sensitive data.
- Report safety defects or pattern errors promptly.
- Accept that moderators may restrict content or accounts to protect members and the platform.

## Not permitted

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, intimidation, or dogpiling.
- Sexual exploitation, non-consensual sexual content, or content involving minors in unsafe contexts.
- Hate speech or promotion of extremist violence.
- Doxxing or attempts to identify a pseudonymous member.
- Spam, deceptive promotion, fake testimonials, fake completion photos, or manipulated ratings.
- Pirated patterns, copied characters, trademark misuse, or unlicensed franchise content.
- Resale or redistribution of paid Tuftlings files.
- Scraping member content or using it to train models without the creator's explicit informed permission.
- Selling access credentials, purchase claims, or entitlements.
- Circumventing blocks, suspensions, age gates, or access controls.
- Unsafe instructions presented as tested or approved.

## Remix permission levels

- Showcase only: others may view the project but may not create a derivative from it.
- Remix with credit: derivatives are allowed with visible attribution and a link to the source.
- Non-commercial remix: derivatives are allowed with credit but cannot be sold.
- Commercial handmade remix: credited derivatives may be used for limited handmade finished items, subject to the source licence.

Permissions never transfer ownership of the source design or paid pattern files.

## Community spaces

- Gallery: completed projects and substantial works in progress.
- Make Logs: materials, tools, modifications, time, difficulty, lessons, and pattern version.
- Pattern Help: questions linked to a precise version and step.
- Pattern Lab: controlled tester cohorts and structured evidence.
- Circles: topic-focused group spaces.
- Challenges: opt-in creative prompts without public popularity leaderboards.

Private direct messages are excluded from version 1.

## Moderation approach

Moderation follows five goals:

1. Stop immediate harm.
2. Preserve evidence privately.
3. Explain decisions when safe and lawful.
4. Apply proportionate action consistently.
5. Provide an appeal route except where doing so would create further risk.

Possible actions include guidance, content labels, content restriction, content removal, feature limits, temporary suspension, permanent removal, and preservation/escalation for legal or safety review.

## Member control

Members can:

- Choose a display name.
- Control project visibility.
- Select remix permissions.
- Block other members.
- Report content or behaviour.
- Export their account data.
- Request account deletion.
- Appeal eligible moderation decisions.
- Disable digests and non-essential notifications.
- Use quiet mode and reduced-motion preferences.

## Age boundary

The first release is for adults aged 18 and older. The age gate is not a substitute for moderation or privacy controls. The platform must not knowingly create profiles for children.

## AI and automation

Any AI-assisted product or community asset must be disclosed where required. AI may help classify reports, detect spam, or prepare drafts, but:

- It cannot issue a permanent ban without human review.
- It cannot declare a physical craft pattern safe or tested.
- Member content is not training data by default.
- Members must be able to challenge consequential automated decisions.

## Changes to this charter

Material changes require a dated revision note. Members should receive reasonable notice before changes that affect content rights, access, privacy, or moderation. Emergency safety changes may take effect immediately and must be documented afterward.
