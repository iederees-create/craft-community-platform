# Tuftlings Physical Pattern Testing Programme

Status: Required launch gate  
Applies to: crochet pattern, sewn-plush pattern, accessory components, and subsequent material revisions

## Non-negotiable rule

No Tuftlings pattern may be described as tested, approved, beginner-friendly, child-safe, or ready for sale solely because software, AI, a PDF renderer, or the designer reviewed it.

At least three independent makers must physically complete each primary construction method before approval.

## Version states

- Draft: internal development only.
- Testing: released only to an approved cohort.
- Approved: physical testing gate passed; not necessarily sold yet.
- Published: approved version available to buyers.
- Retired: no longer distributed; history retained.
- Safety hold: access paused pending investigation.

## Cohort composition

For each primary method, recruit at least:

- One advanced beginner
- One intermediate maker
- One experienced maker or pattern tester

Avoid having all testers use the same yarn/fabric, hook/needle, device, printer, or regional paper size.

## Tester application fields

- Display name and secure contact route
- Country/region
- Confirmation of age 18+
- Craft method and experience level
- Accessibility needs
- Yarn/fabric and tools available
- Ability to photograph stages
- Agreement to confidentiality during testing
- Permission choice for finished-project images
- Conflict-of-interest disclosure
- Compensation or benefit offered

## Test protocol

1. Assign an immutable pattern version.
2. Record tester, method, materials, tool size, printer settings, and start time.
3. Require the tester to follow the written instructions before consulting designer clarification.
4. Record every ambiguity at the exact step.
5. Record stitch counts or template dimensions.
6. Photograph critical joins and finished dimensions.
7. Report material consumption.
8. Record total active making time separately from waiting/drying time.
9. Complete safety and usability checklist.
10. Submit an overall assessment without being pressured toward a positive review.

## Required evidence

### Crochet

- Gauge swatch or measurement
- Stitch count at every shaped round
- Material amount
- Finished height, width, and weight
- Join and attachment stability
- Stuffing behaviour
- Eye/accessory security
- Whether each abbreviation and technique is explained
- UK/US terminology consistency

### Sewing

- Printer scale verification at 100%
- A4 and US Letter test
- Test-square measurement
- Seam allowance confirmation
- Piece labelling and grain direction
- Cutting count
- Turning gaps
- Alignment marks
- Finished dimensions
- Attachment security

## Severity of findings

- Blocking: cannot complete, unsafe construction, materially wrong dimensions, missing pieces, impossible stitch count.
- Major: substantial ambiguity, repeated correction required, misleading skill level, significant material variance.
- Minor: typo, layout issue, optional clarification, non-blocking visual inconsistency.
- Suggestion: preference or enhancement not required for correctness.

Blocking and major findings require a new pattern version and re-test of affected steps.

## Approval gate

A version can become approved only when:

- Three independent completions exist for each primary method.
- No unresolved blocking issue exists.
- Major issues have been corrected and rechecked.
- Stitch-count and template-dimension validation passes.
- A4 and US Letter outputs have been printed and measured.
- Safety warnings match actual construction.
- Finished-size ranges are based on evidence.
- Accessibility review is complete.
- The owner signs the release record.

## Tester acknowledgement

Only testers who consent may be named. Acknowledgement is not a testimonial. Do not rewrite critical feedback into promotional copy.

## Pattern update process

When a published issue is confirmed:

1. Create an erratum linked to the affected version and step.
2. Classify safety impact.
3. Correct the source, not only the exported PDF.
4. Increment the version.
5. Re-run automated validation.
6. Physically re-test affected construction.
7. Notify entitled buyers when the correction is material.
8. Keep an accurate changelog.

## Prohibited claims before approval

Do not publish:

- Tested by real makers
- Beginner guaranteed
- Child-safe
- No-fail pattern
- Perfect stitch counts
- Professionally tested
- Suitable for any yarn or fabric

The listing may truthfully state that a prototype is in an active Pattern Lab, but a prototype must not be sold as a finished pattern.
