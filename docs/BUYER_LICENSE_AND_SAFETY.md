# Tuftlings Buyer Licence and Safety Draft

Status: Draft for legal and physical-product review  
This document is operational guidance, not legal advice.

## Digital file licence

A purchase grants one individual buyer a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use the supplied Tuftlings pattern files for personal crafting and the limited handmade use described below.

The buyer may:

- Download and store a personal working copy.
- Print copies for personal use.
- Create finished handmade items.
- Modify a finished item for personal creative expression.
- Share photographs of finished projects with appropriate pattern credit.
- Sell limited quantities of finished items made personally by the buyer, if the final release explicitly enables commercial handmade use.

The buyer may not:

- Resell, redistribute, upload, email, share, or publish the pattern files.
- Sell modified versions of the pattern.
- Convert the files into another format for redistribution.
- Include the files in a bundle, class library, membership archive, or generative-AI dataset.
- Manufacture finished items at industrial or factory scale.
- Use Tuftlings branding in a way that implies endorsement, partnership, or official merchandise status.
- Register the pattern, character artwork, or branding as their own intellectual property.
- Share community access or purchase entitlements.

## Finished-item attribution

Recommended wording:

Handmade by [maker name] using an original Tuftlings pattern by NextGen Webs.

Attribution does not create a partnership or transfer ownership.

## Community content

A member retains ownership of original project photographs, notes, and modifications they upload. By posting, the member grants the platform only the permissions required to store, display, moderate, back up, and deliver that content according to their visibility settings.

A remix permission applies to the project contribution; it does not grant access to paid source files.

Member content must not be used to train generative models without separate, informed, opt-in permission.

## Digital-product notice

- This is a digital product; no physical item is shipped.
- Software, printer, paper, yarn, fabric, filling, tools, and hardware are not included unless explicitly stated.
- Colours and final sizes vary with materials, tension, printing, displays, and maker technique.
- Download and refund terms must match the active Etsy policy and applicable consumer law.

## General craft safety

- Patterns use needles, hooks, pins, scissors, and small components.
- Keep tools and loose materials away from children and animals.
- Follow manufacturer instructions for yarn, fabric, filling, glue, eyes, fasteners, and tools.
- Inspect finished items regularly for loose seams or parts.
- Discontinue use if an item is damaged.
- Handmade variation can affect strength and suitability.

## Babies, children, and pets

Do not describe a finished object as a baby toy, child-safe, pet-safe, sleep-safe, or compliant with a product-safety standard without appropriate material selection, physical testing, labelling, and jurisdiction-specific compliance.

Safety eyes are not automatically safe for children. Buttons, beads, bells, magnets, detachable accessories, cords, and poorly secured embroidery may present choking, ingestion, strangulation, or injury hazards.

Where the pattern is used decoratively, say so plainly.

## Allergies and materials

The maker is responsible for checking material composition, dyes, finishes, adhesives, fillings, and cross-contamination relevant to the intended recipient.

Do not claim hypoallergenic, non-toxic, organic, flame-resistant, washable, food-safe, or medical suitability without evidence from the actual materials and construction.

## Seller responsibility for finished items

A buyer who sells finished items becomes responsible for:

- Applicable product-safety rules
- Accurate material and age labelling
- Quality control
- Consumer rights
- Tax and business obligations
- Insurance where appropriate
- Avoiding misleading claims

The digital pattern seller cannot certify each buyer's materials or workmanship.

## AI disclosure

Where AI materially assisted images, text, pattern development, or listing assets, use an accurate disclosure consistent with Etsy rules. AI review does not replace physical testing.

## Finalisation checklist

Before including this licence in buyer files:

- Confirm the public brand and rights holder.
- Confirm whether limited commercial handmade sales are enabled.
- Define a reasonable quantity or scale boundary if used.
- Review South African and target-market consumer requirements.
- Align refunds and support language with the current Etsy listing.
- Confirm support contact.
- Obtain legal review for material commercial use.
